The newly appointed secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, Mohsen Rezaei, stated to Iranian state media that he will prevent the flow of oil through the Gulf should countries in the region join the new US economic sanctions campaign against Tehran, treating any joiner as an 'enemy'. The report still lacks independent corroboration, but fits into a documented pattern of using the Strait of Hormuz as Iran's main deterrent lever — about 20-30% of globally traded oil transits through the route. The threat emerges on the same day the US Treasury promises 'economic asphyxiation' of Iran, with sanctions on Chinese firms linked to Tehran, and China publicly warns against unilateral sanctions without a basis in the UN Security Council.
